# Fileship trademark policy

The Fileship code is open source. The Fileship name and logo are not part of that license: they identify the official Fileship project, so people can tell it apart from other software. This policy says how you can use them.

## You can, without asking

- Say that your software or service is **based on Fileship**, **powered by Fileship** or **compatible with Fileship**.
- Write about Fileship, review it, teach it and link to it, in articles, videos, talks and documentation.
- Use the name to refer to Fileship accurately, for example "we host Fileship for our team" or "a ShareX uploader for Fileship".
- Keep the Fileship name and logo in an unmodified copy of Fileship that you host or distribute.

## You need permission for

- Naming your product, service, company or domain "Fileship", or something that could be confused with it, such as "Fileship Pro", "Fileship Hosting" or "MyFileship".
- Offering a modified version of Fileship under the Fileship name. Give it a name of your own, and say that it is based on Fileship.
- Using the Fileship logo, or a logo that looks like it, as the logo of your own product or service.
- Suggesting that your product or service is official, certified or endorsed by the Fileship project.
- Selling merchandise with the Fileship name or logo.

To ask, write to contact@hanzy.dev.

## Attribution is different

The "Powered by Fileship" notice that the [license](LICENSING.md#additional-term-attribution) requires on the login page is a use of the name that is always allowed. It does not need permission, and it may not be removed.
