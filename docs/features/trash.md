# Trash

Deleted files, folders and notes go to the **Trash** first, where they stay for the instance's retention period (30 days by default). Each item shows when it will be deleted forever.

- **Restore** brings an item back. A restored folder brings back what was deleted with it, and an item whose folder is still in the trash goes to the top level.
- **Delete forever** removes the selected items, and **Empty trash** removes everything. This cannot be undone.
- The tabs **Files**, **Folders** and **Notes** separate the kinds of items.
- Right after a delete, **Undo** in the notification restores it.
- To skip the trash, choose **Delete forever** in the delete confirmation.

Items in the trash still count toward your storage limit. Short links are deleted right away and never go to the trash. Expired files and files that reach their view limit are deleted for good, without the trash.

Admins set the retention period; see [Trash and retention](../configuration/trash-and-retention.md).
